Assertions required for MBOE to connect the MVF metamodel with skos:concept
The MVF.xmi has missing elements that are present in the PDF spec - see MVF Metamodel - Per XMI (missing elements). The MVF specification PDF provides additional information about the equivalence of skos:Concept and MVFEntry. The full MVF metamodel based on the imported AboutMvf.rdf specification is at MVF Metamodel - Per RDF, which again differs from the XMI and PDF.

Neither the XMI or PDF asserts the equivalence of Concept and MVFEntry. This diagram provides the missing assertions.
