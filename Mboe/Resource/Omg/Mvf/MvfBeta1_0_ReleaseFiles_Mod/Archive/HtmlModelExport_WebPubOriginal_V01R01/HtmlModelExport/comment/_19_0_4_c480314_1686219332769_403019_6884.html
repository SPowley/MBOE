Applied Stereotype  Literal Annotation
Annotated Element  VocabularyEntryStatus
Body The MVF Spec does not dictate what values should be adopted for entry status. The enumerated values here are based on the idea that a term goes through the following life-cycle (in order):
DICTIONARY_DEF - a term is in use in a context but no formal definition of the corresponding concept has been proposed or adopted for the relevant context, relying on dictionary definitions
PROPOSED - term and definition have been proposed but not approved
DRAFT - a term and definition have been provisionally accepted, subject to an approval process
PUBLISHED - term and definition are approved and published in an official document (note: considered as official in the context, may not be official in other contexts)
WITHDRAWN - term no longer in use i.e. officially withdrawn This possibly duplicates isDeprecated in some contexts, but not necessarily - for example a concept from a standard may remain in use on a project after the standard is withdrawn because it was current when the product was developed.
