Property status

Comment

Link to an optional rating established from a predetermined scale and used to evaluate the standing of a term (vocabulary entry) in the context of a given vocabulary.

Note that this attribute is labeled status in Section 8.7 of the PDF specification, termStatus in Section 9.1 of the PDF and mvfEntryStatus in the XMI. The equivalence of these attributes is assumed for this model.

[Source: www.omg.org/spec/MVF/1.0/Beta1/PDF]
